# A time limit on every job

## Why
A hung cache save blocked the only Linux runner for 30 min on 24 Nov. A job that hangs must end soon, not hold the runner.

## What
- `timeout-minutes` at job level in this repository's workflows: 20 for test, check and lint jobs, 60 for release jobs that build images.
- A job that calls a reusable workflow takes its limit from there (the shared workflows in nayweelabs/.github).
