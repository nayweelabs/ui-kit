# ci: no setup-node cache on self-hosted runners

## Why
CI on our self-hosted runners hangs in the "Post Run actions/setup-node" step: the Actions cache save never finishes, and that blocks the only Linux runner.

## What
Remove `cache: npm` from the `actions/setup-node` step in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. npm's cache stays on the runner.

## Done when
- No `actions/setup-node` step has `cache:` set.
- The PR's own CI run finishes without hanging in the post step.

None.
