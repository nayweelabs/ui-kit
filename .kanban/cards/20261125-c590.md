# License badge and panel in the top bar

## Why
- Owner, 25 Nov: a license badge is standard for every product UI, always visible in the top bar, free for everyone.
- Exactly three words: Free, Trial, Licensed. The word never changes for details; only the colour turns to warn when attention is needed.
- Information is free for all. Push (bell, CLI start line, events, notices) is the enterprise part (thresholds, ADR-S23, later), not this card.

## What
- One ui-kit component, badge plus panel: a top-bar item in `ui/bar` (`partials/shell.html`, a field on `kit.Bar` in `types.go`), like a status light (`ui/light`) opening a popover.
- It reads only the mankit/v1 `license show` answer (`kit.License` in `license.go`): `state`, `licenses[].trialEnds`, `offerEnds`, `daysLeft`, `for`, and `features`. No logic in the page.
- Word from `state`:
  - none, trial-ended and ended → Free;
  - trial → Trial;
  - licensed and ending → Licensed.
- Warn colour: ending; trial with `daysLeft` ≤ 3; ended (ADR-L3's notice stays until the license is replaced).
- The badge words replace today's `ui/license-badge` words (Enterprise, Ended) in `partials/license.html`, so the Settings card and the bar say the same word.
- A click opens the License panel: the content of Settings "License and features" (`ui/license`), with the owner's texts, each from the answer's fields:
  - "Trial: N days left (ends <date>)", "1 day" in the singular;
  - "Your trial has ended. Buy a license to keep the paid features.";
  - "This version doesn't include a free trial anymore. Download the latest version to try <App>." (a release-trial whose offer ended);
  - "Licensed to <for> · <App>".
- The panel also shows what the license opens (the features) and the add license / buy actions.
- Go partial, React wrapper (`react/index.js`, `react/index.d.ts`), a case per state in `preview/cases.go` with goldens, README's License section.
- The TUI shows the same word in its status line (not in ui-kit; see Open).
