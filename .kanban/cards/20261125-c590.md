# License badge and panel in the top bar

## Why
- Owner, 25 Nov: a license badge is standard for every product UI, always visible in the top bar, free for everyone.
- Exactly three words: Free, Trial, Licensed. The word never changes for details; only the colour turns to warn when attention is needed.
- Information is free for all. Push (bell, CLI start line, events, notices) is the enterprise part (thresholds, ADR-S23, later), not this card.

## What
- One ui-kit component, badge plus panel: a top-bar item in `ui/bar` (`partials/shell.html`, a field on `kit.Bar` in `types.go`), like a status light (`ui/light`) opening a popover.
- It reads only the mankit/v1 `license show` answer (`kit.License` in `license.go`): `state`, `licenses[].trialEnds`, `offerEnds`, `until` (for a trial key), `daysLeft`, `for`, and `features`. No logic in the page.
- Add `TrialEnds` and `OfferEnds` to `kit.LicenseFile` (`license.go`) and to `react/index.d.ts`.
- Word from `state`:
  - none, trial-ended and ended → Free;
  - trial → Trial;
  - licensed and ending → Licensed.
- Warn colour: ending; trial with `daysLeft` ≤ 3; trial-ended; ended (owner, 25 Nov: Free in warn after a trial or a paid license ends).
- `ui/license-badge` (`partials/license.html`) becomes the one badge template, used by both the Settings card (`ui/license`) and the bar, with its words (today Enterprise, Ended) replaced by Free, Trial, Licensed.
- A click opens the License panel: the content of Settings "License and features" (`ui/license`): the sentence, what the license opens (the features), and the add license action; no buy.
- The panel shows the sentence the answer gives; it builds no text. The four sentences and the choice of which one shows live in mankit's `licenses/cli` `says()`.
- <App> is the app's `serve.App.Title`, the name the page already shows in `kit.Bar.App`; the page passes it.
- Go partial, React wrapper (`react/index.js`, `react/index.d.ts`), a case per state in `preview/cases.go` with goldens, README's License section.
- The TUI shows the same word in its status line (not in ui-kit; see Open).
- Not in this card: the buy link (purchase MVP).

## Done when

- [ ] A page uses it with no logic of its own.
