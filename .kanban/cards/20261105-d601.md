# The confirm step does not work in Safari

The owner, 5 Nov 2026, in Safari, on control-room's Settings → GitHub (test build on :7811): pressing Connect on the read-token form went to `/github/connect` and the server answered "Nothing was changed: this change asks first, and asking needs JavaScript". So the form was sent without `confirm=yes`: kit.js did not ask, or asked and sent the form without the mark. In Chrome both a click and Enter ask and send `confirm=yes` (tested with Playwright).

Likely place: `js/kit.js` `send()`: it adds the hidden `confirm` input, calls `requestSubmit`, then removes the input at once in `finally`. If Safari builds the form data after that, the mark is gone. The same pattern may be why "Open the board / Open the design" did nothing in Safari on 4 Nov.

Done when: reproduced in WebKit (Playwright), fixed, a test covers it, and a click and Enter both send `confirm=yes` in WebKit and Chrome; control-room picks up the ui-kit release.
