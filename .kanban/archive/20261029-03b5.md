#feature #v1 #road-r6

# A section's Read again shows it is busy, then Updated, and when it was read

Owner: pressing a section's Read again gave no clear feedback, and nothing said how old the section's data is.

## Plan

- While it reloads: the button spins and is disabled, the section dims under a thin accent bar (kit tokens, calm under reduced motion). Then "Updated" beside the button for 4 seconds, announced through aria-live. A failure shows the section's error box as now.
- `ui/read-at` (kit.ReadAt): "read 10:42" with the exact time on hover. A lazy section draws it empty and kit.js fills it from the UI-Read-At header of the section's HTML, so it is the server's read time; `Section.Read` and `Bar.Read` draw it on the server.
- Goldens, kit.js tests, README.
